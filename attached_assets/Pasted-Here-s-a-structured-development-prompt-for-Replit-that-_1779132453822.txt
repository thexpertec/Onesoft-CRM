## **Replit Development Prompt – Customer Ledger Wallet & Auto Payment Adjustment**

### **Objective**

Develop a customer ledger system where:

1. Customer ledger acts as a **wallet**.
2. Extra payments are automatically saved as wallet/advance.
3. Wallet balance is auto-adjusted against future invoices or POS bills.
4. Sale returns and refunds adjust wallet or outstanding invoices.
5. Payment can be collected from:

   * POS Payments
   * Sale Invoice Payment
   * Journal Entry
   * Receipt Voucher

---

### **Core Features**

#### **1. Customer Ledger / Wallet**

* Each customer ledger shows:

  * Debit = Customer owes (outstanding)
  * Credit = Wallet / advance balance
* Wallet balance is automatically applied against new invoices or POS sales.
* Ledger should maintain a full **transaction history**:

  * Invoice creation
  * Payments applied
  * Wallet adjustments
  * Sale returns

#### **2. Payment Allocation Engine**

* Payment allocation logic should be **common across all modules**:

  1. Adjust against selected invoice if payment is invoice-specific.
  2. Adjust against oldest unpaid invoices.
  3. Remaining goes to wallet/advance.
* Prevent over-application:

  * Wallet cannot go negative
  * Invoice cannot be overpaid; extra amount goes to wallet

#### **3. Sale Invoice Module**

* On creating a new invoice:

  * Auto-apply wallet first
  * Remaining amount becomes outstanding
* Payment scenarios:

  * Wallet >= invoice → mark invoice as **paid**, reduce wallet
  * Wallet < invoice → adjust wallet, remaining as due
* Accounting entries:

  * DR Accounts Receivable / Customer
  * CR Sales
  * Wallet adjustment if any: DR Wallet / Customer Advance → CR Customer / Accounts Receivable

#### **4. POS Module**

* POS sale auto-checks customer wallet.
* Payment scenarios:

  * Wallet covers full bill → mark POS paid, reduce wallet
  * Wallet covers partial → apply wallet, customer pays remainder
  * Extra payment → add to wallet
* Accounting entries same as Sale Invoice, including tax if applicable

#### **5. Sale Return Module**

* On sale return:

  1. Reduce outstanding first
  2. Excess return goes to wallet or refund
* Return scenarios:

  * Original invoice unpaid → reduce invoice outstanding
  * Original invoice fully paid → add to wallet or refund
  * Partially paid → adjust outstanding and wallet accordingly
* Accounting entries:

  * DR Sale Return
  * CR Customer / Accounts Receivable or Wallet

#### **6. Receipt Voucher**

* Payment collection screen must:

  * Show invoice total, outstanding, wallet balance
  * Auto-adjust wallet by default
* If payment > outstanding → remaining goes to wallet
* Accounting entries:

  * DR Cash / Bank
  * CR Customer / Accounts Receivable (adjusted)
  * CR Customer Advance / Wallet (if extra)

#### **7. Journal Entry**

* Used for manual adjustment:

  * Opening balances
  * Manual wallet entries
  * Customer corrections
* JE must require:

  * Customer selection
  * Adjustment type (wallet / outstanding)
  * Reason / remarks
* JE allocation can optionally adjust invoices

### **Business Rules**

1. Wallet applied automatically on any new invoice or POS sale.
2. Payment over outstanding → extra goes to wallet.
3. Returns first reduce outstanding, excess to wallet or refund.
4. Invoice status rules:

   * Unpaid / Partially Paid / Paid / Overpaid / Returned / Partially Returned
5. Ledger must always reconcile:

   * Outstanding + Wallet = net customer balance
6. JE or Receipt Voucher must respect allocation engine.

---

### **Example Flow**

1. Customer has outstanding 100
2. Customer pays 200 → 100 applied, 100 saved to wallet
3. New invoice 5000 → 100 wallet applied, 4900 outstanding
4. Sale return 500 → reduces outstanding or adds to wallet
5. POS payment uses wallet automatically, extra goes to wallet

### **Technical Notes**

* Implement a **common allocation engine** to be called from:

  * POS Payments
  * Sale Invoice Payment
  * Journal Entry
  * Receipt Voucher
  * Sale Return
* Ensure **atomicity**: payment adjustment, wallet update, invoice status update must be **transactional**.
* Provide APIs / functions for:

  * Get customer wallet balance
  * Apply wallet to invoice
  * Record wallet / advance movement
  * Generate customer ledger report
