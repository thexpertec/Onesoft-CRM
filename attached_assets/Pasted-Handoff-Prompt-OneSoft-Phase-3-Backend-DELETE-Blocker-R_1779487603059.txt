  client: import("pg").PoolClient,
  tenantId: string,
  before: Record<string, unknown>,

The hook runs inside the DELETE transaction, after SELECT … FOR UPDATE, before the soft/hard delete branch. Non-empty result → ROLLBACK + 409 with Cannot delete <entityType> <name|code|id>: <blockers.join(", ")>. Remove the underlying records first. Applies to both soft and ?hard=1 paths.
