### Column Logic Details

1. **#**  
   - Auto-incrementing row number.

2. **Colour**  
   - A small color picker or colored badge (e.g., role color or department color).  
   - Default to a soft gray if not set.

3. **Role**  
   - Text field (editable).  
   - Example: Admin, Manager, Staff.

4. **Department**  
   - Text field or dropdown (pre-populated with existing dept list).  
   - Allow adding new departments inline.

5. **Designation**  
   - Text field (editable).  
   - Example: Senior Software Engineer, Team Lead.

6. **Description**  
   - Multi-line text field or short text input.

7. **Head of Department** — *special logic*  
   - If the department has **exactly 1 staff member** → display that staff’s name as plain text (non-editable in this column).  
   - If the department has **2 or more staff members** → show a **dropdown list** of all staff members in that department, allowing the user to select one as Head of Department.  
   - If the department has **0 staff members** → show “—”.

8. **Permissions**  
   - A comma-separated list of permission names (editable) or a small modal/popup to assign permissions.

9. **Staff**  
   - List of staff members assigned to that Role/Department/Designation combo.  
   - Display as avatars, names, or a count with a tooltip.

10. **Action**  
    - Edit and Delete icons/buttons.
