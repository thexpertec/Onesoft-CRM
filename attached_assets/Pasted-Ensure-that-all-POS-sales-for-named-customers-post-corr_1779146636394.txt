Ensure that all POS sales for named customers post correctly to:

Customer AR Ledger (receivable)
Cash / Bank Ledger (payment collected)
Revenue Ledger
COGS / Inventory Ledger
Customer Advance / Wallet Ledger (if customer pays extra)
Requirements
Customer AR line is mandatory for named customers
Walk-in customers: continue using the existing "transit embedded" JE flow.
Named customer: AR ledger must appear in JE for both sale and payment portions.
Wallet/Advance handling
If the customer has an existing wallet balance → auto-apply to the invoice/sale.
If customer pays more than sale total → excess → credit customer advance (wallet).
JE should reflect wallet application as a separate line:
DR Customer Advance Ledger    appliedWalletAmount
CR Customer AR Ledger         appliedWalletAmount
Cash / Bank line
Only includes the actual cash received after wallet adjustment.
For example: Sale = 500, Wallet applied = 100, Cash paid = 400 → DR Cash 400.
Revenue / COGS / Inventory
Continue posting as before:
DR Cost of Goods Sold         cogsAmount
CR Inventory                  cogsAmount

DR Customer AR / Cash (total sale)
CR Revenue                    saleAmount
Reference / Narration
JE reference should indicate POS sale + customer ID + timestamp.
JE lines should have descriptive narration: e.g., "POS Sale SAL-202605-00001 – Customer PAKISTAN".
Balance check
Total DR = Total CR
Customer ledger should show the sale and wallet adjustments clearly.
Flow Example

Scenario:

Sale total = 500
Customer wallet = 100
Customer pays 450 cash → total received = 550 (overpayment)

JE lines should be:

Ledger	DR	CR	Notes
Customer AR	500	0	Record sale receivable
Revenue	0	500	Record sale revenue
Cash / Bank	450	0	Cash collected
Customer AR	0	450	Offset AR with cash
Customer Advance / Wallet	50	0	Extra received → credit wallet
Customer AR	0	50	Offset AR with extra (to wallet)
COGS	200	0	Cost of goods sold
Inventory	0	200	Reduce inventory

Notes:

Wallet application is recorded separately.
Cash received is adjusted after wallet.
AR is always debited first, then credited with cash & wallet usage.
Implementation Checklist
Fetch named customer and ensure advanceLedgerAccountId exists.
Calculate wallet available using getCustomerWalletBalance(customerId).
Calculate applied wallet amount: min(walletBalance, saleTotal).
Calculate actual cash collected: saleTotal - appliedWalletAmount.
Post JE with the following lines:
- DR Customer AR           totalSaleAmount
- CR Revenue               totalSaleAmount
- DR Cash / Bank           cashReceived
- CR Customer AR           cashReceived
- DR Customer Advance      appliedWalletAmount
- CR Customer AR           appliedWalletAmount
- DR COGS                  cogsAmount
- CR Inventory             cogsAmount
Ensure JE reference prefixes match your system standards (e.g., AUTO-SAL-YYYYMM-XXXX).
Validate total DR = total CR before posting.
Update customer ledger report and wallet balance after posting.
Log any overpayment to ensure wallet balance is updated.
Additional Safeguards
Walk-in customer → skip AR / Wallet lines; keep transit embedded.
If cash + wallet does not cover sale total → invoice remains partially unpaid.
Wallet applied should never exceed available wallet balance.
Post a single JE per POS sale to avoid duplicate entries.
Add unit tests / simulation with combinations:
No wallet, wallet < sale, wallet = sale, wallet > sale, overpayment.

This ensures:

Customer ledger is always updated
Wallet / advance credit handled correctly
Double-entry accounting maintained
POS sale shows correctly in reports
Extra amounts are posted to wallet automatically
