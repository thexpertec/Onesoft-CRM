Frontend View for Calculation-Based Invoicing
Here’s a simplified frontend user interface design for the invoicing page. The following elements will be visible to the user when they are entering product details, generating amounts, and viewing the invoice.
________________________________________
1. Product Selection
•	Dropdown Menu:
o	Allow the user to choose from different products
2. Product Details Entry
•	Dynamic Input Fields:
o	Item Name: Automatically populated based on the product selection.
o	For Size-Based Products (e.g., White Sheet):
	Height (in inches/feet)
	Width (in inches/feet)
	Automatically calculate the total size based on these dimensions.
o	Unit Rate:
	Display the rate for the selected product (this will be filled by the system based on the selection).
________________________________________
3. Additional Charges
•	Service Charges (Optional):
o	Labour Charges: Field to enter labor costs (e.g., Shet Cutting or Labour).
o	Other Charges: Field for entering any other additional costs (e.g., Power Supply).
________________________________________
4. Amount Calculation
•	Auto-Calculation:
o	Amount for Size-Based Products: Display the calculated amount (e.g., Rate x Total Size).
o	Amount for Quantity-Based Products: Display the calculated amount (e.g., Quantity x Rate).
________________________________________
5. Subtotal
•	Subtotal Display:
o	Automatically sum up the amounts of all products and additional charges.
________________________________________
6. Tax Application
•	Tax Rate Input (Optional):
o	Allow user to input tax percentage (e.g., 5% VAT) or automatically apply if predefined.
o	Auto-Calculated Tax: Display the calculated tax based on the subtotal.
________________________________________
7. Final Total
•	Final Total:
o	Display the final total amount after adding the tax and any additional charges.
________________________________________
8. Invoice Preview
•	Invoice Layout:
o	Show a preview of the invoice with the following fields:
	Invoice Number
	Client Information: Name, address, contact details.
	Product Information: Item name, dimensions/quantity, rate, amount.
	Subtotal: Total before tax.
	Tax: Applied tax amount.
	Total: Final amount including tax.
	Payment Terms: Display payment terms (e.g., "Due on receipt", "30 days net").
________________________________________
9. Export Options
•	Generate PDF/Excel: Buttons to allow users to export the invoice in PDF or Excel format.
